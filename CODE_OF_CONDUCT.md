# Code of conduct

SafeGuard Sentinel contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
safe, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and open to correction.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and different levels of technical experience.
- Use synthetic examples and minimize sensitive detail in all public spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publishing another person's sensitive information are
not acceptable.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Open an issue titled `Conduct
contact request` without names or incident details. A maintainer will arrange a
private channel. Immediate threats should be reported to the relevant platform
or local authority.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.
